HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1

    THE JOB

    Congratulations, boss. Someone sold you a struggling company and left four quarters to clean up the mess.

  2. 2

    THE LEVERS

    Each quarter you set five controls before opening the doors:

    • PRICE: Selling price per unit. Cheaper chases bargain hunters; pricier protects unit margin.
    • QUALITY: Better materials cost more per unit, but win Mainstream and picky Premium shoppers.
    • QUANTITY: Units built this quarter. Unsold stock spoils or carries over based on company traits.
    • AUDIENCE: Aims your storefront at Budget, Mainstream, or Premium to win more of them.
    • MARKETING: Lifts demand across every segment, with diminishing returns if you burn too much cash.
  3. 3

    THE RIVALS

    Bot competitors fight you for the same wallets. Watch their prices and which customer crowd they are chasing.

  4. 4

    THE SURPRISES

    Quarterly market events shake up demand or costs, and incoming memos between quarters force deals that can help or haunt later quarters.

  5. 5

    THE SCORE

    Your score is total profit % across all four quarters, scaled on positive returns by difficulty (x0.7 to x1.8). One scored daily run per day; Unscored Overtime is purely for practice.

-- Marge: "Try not to bankrupt us before lunch. I've already updated my CV."
